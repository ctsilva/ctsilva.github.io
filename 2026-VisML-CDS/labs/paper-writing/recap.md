### Slides

The slides used this week can be found [here](https://ctsilva.github.io/2026-VisML-CDS/labs/paper-writing/lab).

### Topics Covered

* The research paper as a persuasive argument, and who you are writing for
* What each section does: abstract, introduction, related works, background, design goals, methods, evaluation, conclusion
* Related works done well: organize by idea, not by paper
* Figure design: no plain screenshots
* Citations with BibTeX
* Optional: other tools for visualizing deep learning (NeuroCartography, ActiVis, Dodrio, GAN Lab, and more)
